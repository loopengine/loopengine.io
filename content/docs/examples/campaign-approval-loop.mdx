<Callout variant="warning" title="Narrative pattern — ABM outbound">
Governed **campaign launch** — not autonomous email generation. Marketing execution platform is **Integration**; humans approve in **Channel**.
</Callout>

## Runtime flow

| Stage | In this pattern |
| --- | --- |
| **Provider** | LLM generates sequence copy, subject lines, and personalization tokens from account research |
| **Loop + Guards** | `campaign.launch` — brand voice guard, compliance keyword blocklist, `evidence-required` |
| **Channel** | **Slack** — marketing ops + legal review in `#campaign-approvals` |
| **Integration** | MAP/SEP (e.g. Outreach, Marketo webhook) — schedule sends only after `APPROVED` |
| **Evidence + learning** | Copy hash, guard outcomes, approval latency; learning on reply-rate vs predicted |

## Operational scenario

1. ABM operator opens loop for target account list segment.
2. **Provider** produces draft sequences per persona — no sends yet.
3. **Guards** block transitions containing forbidden claims or missing disclaimers.
4. **Channel** collects approve / revise / reject from brand and legal roles.
5. **Integration** activates campaign in marketing platform with approved artifact id.
6. **Evidence** supports regulatory review (“who approved this copy, when, with what model”).

## Deterministic boundaries

- Prompt instructions cannot bypass compliance **guards**.
- Slack reactions are not sends — MAP activation is post-governance **Integration**.
